(e) A borrower may prepay the loan in full or, with the consent of the bank or trust company, may refinance the loan. If the interest is calculated on the actuarial basis, or if the evidence of the indebtedness provides that the rate of interest may vary from time to time, a borrower may prepay the loan in full without penalty.
